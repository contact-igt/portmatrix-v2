# PORTMATRIX project rules

- The approved PORTMATRIX PDF is the factual and content authority; reuse the existing source maps rather than remapping it.
- Do not invent claims, statistics, testimonials, credentials, captions, or identities of people in photographs.
- Treat genuine PORTMATRIX imagery as contextual trust evidence. Follow the PDF/image map, and never redraw, recreate, or stylize the official logo.
- Keep code minimal and reusable; do not add dependencies unless genuinely necessary.
- Maintain a premium, evidence-led editorial design: no generic SaaS/AI-template styling, excessive card walls, glassmorphism, or gradients.
- Preserve natural typography proportions. Use Manrope for headings and Inter for body, UI, navigation, tables, and captions.
- Visually verify desktop, tablet, and mobile. Keep the mobile menu accessible and working.
- Work locally only; do not deploy. Verify source accuracy, assets, responsiveness, and build status before declaring completion.

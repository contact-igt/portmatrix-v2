Phase 5: Shipment Procedures




                                                                                         13
1.​ Export Documentation
●​ Prepare and verify all required export documents, such as the Commercial
    Invoice, Packing List, Shipping Bill, Certificate of Origin, Transport Documents,
    and applicable Certificates.



2.​ Filing of Shipping Bill
●​ Submit the Shipping Bill electronically to Customs with accurate details of the
    exporter, goods, value, classification, scheme benefits, and supporting
    documents for export clearance.



3.​ Duty Drawback Entitlement
●​ Claim eligible duty drawback benefits to obtain a refund of specified duties on
    inputs used in the manufacture or processing of exported goods, subject to
    applicable rules and rates; U/S 75 of The Customs Act, 1962.



4.​ Customs Clearance & Examination
●​ Customs verifies the export documents, assesses the goods and compliance
    requirements, and conducts examination where applicable before permitting the
    shipment to proceed.



5.​ Obtaining Let Export Order (LEO)
●​ Obtain the Customs Let Export Order (LEO) after successful document
    verification and examination, authorizing the goods to be exported.


                                                                                  14
   6.​ Loading of Goods to the Conveyance
   ●​ Load the cleared export goods onto the designated vessel, aircraft, vehicle, or
       other conveyance after obtaining the required Customs clearance and LEO.



   7.​ Obtaining Shipping Bill / Bill of Export
   ●​ Obtain the duly processed Shipping Bill or Bill of Export from Customs as the
       official record confirming the clearance and export of the goods.

Flow Chart: Export Documentation → Filing of Shipping Bill → Duty Drawback
Entitlement → Customs Clearance & Examination → Obtaining Let Export Order (LEO)
→ Loading of Goods into Conveyance → Obtaining Final Shipping Bill / Bill of Export.

 Baggage refers to personal belongings, travel articles, and other goods carried by
 passengers while entering or leaving India. Baggage includes unaccompanied
 baggage and excludes motor vehicles. Passengers arriving in India are allowed to
 bring bonafide (used personal effects) and specified goods; free of customs duty
 within prescribed limits.

Baggage Clearance

Every passenger entering India has to pass through a Customs check after first being
cleared by an immigration Officer and taking delivery of his / her baggage, if any, from
the conveyor belts. The passenger has the option of seeking Customs clearance
through either of the two channels: a) Green Channel b) Red Channel. When the
passenger chooses Green Channel, it is deemed to be declared that there is nothing
dutiable or restricted or prohibited goods in his baggage. In the Red Channel, the goods
are made available for inspection by the Customs Authorities.

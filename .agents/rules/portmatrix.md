# PORTMATRIX Project Rules

- The approved PORTMATRIX PDF (`docs/source/PORTMATRIX-website-source.pdf`) and existing source maps (`docs/PORTMATRIX_IMAGE_MAP.md`, `docs/PORTMATRIX_ASSET_MANIFEST.md`, `docs/SOURCE_COVERAGE_MATRIX.md`) are the sole factual and content authority.
- Do not invent or hallucinate claims, statistics, credentials, captions, or identities of people.
- The official PORTMATRIX logo must never be recreated, redrawn, or retyped; use only the authoritative source asset.
- Genuine PORTMATRIX photos serve as documentary trust evidence; treat them with historical and contextual integrity.
- Every image must stay tied to its documented source context; do not use random filler or stock images where real evidence exists.
- Source-derived text shortening may simplify wording for layout fit, but must preserve original intent, scope, and factual accuracy.
- Typography proportions: Manrope for headings and Inter for body, UI, navigation, tables, and captions.
- Design Dials:
  - `DESIGN_VARIANCE=8`
  - `MOTION_INTENSITY=3`
  - `VISUAL_DENSITY=7`
- Preserve existing SEO architecture, metadata structures, and route mappings.
- Keep code minimal, modular, and reusable; do not add unnecessary dependencies.
- Work locally only; never deploy to external hosting or remote servers.
- Visually and programmatically verify changes before declaring completion.

# UI Design & Editorial Taste

## Purpose
Enforce premium, high-trust, editorial design standards tailored for an institutional compliance brand.

## Rules
- **Premium non-template UI**: Avoid cookie-cutter layouts; build bespoke, editorial visual structures.
- **Strong hierarchy**: Establish clear typographic and visual contrast to guide reading focus.
- **Controlled density**: Balance high informational density with structured grid spacing.
- **Editorial composition**: Use asymmetric, photo-led, and magazine-style content arrangements.
- **No generic centered SaaS hero**: Avoid centered headline + twin buttons + floating screenshot hero layouts.
- **No gradient text**: Maintain solid, high-legibility typographic fills.
- **No glassmorphism**: Avoid blurry backdrop filters, frosted glass overlays, and faux-3D sheen.
- **No card walls**: Avoid repetitive grids of identical rounded cards with drop shadows.
- **No animation for decoration**: Motion must serve content structure, state changes, or user guidance.
- **Content-driven composition**: Page structure must directly mirror the nature and narrative of its specific content.
